What no one tells you about landing the role

After years building an international career, I learned the path is different from what they teach you. These are the truths that change the game — and that become strategy in the mentoring:

Truth 01

Certifications don't get you hired. Your portfolio does.

Recruiters want to see what you've built — a real cloud architecture, a pipeline that works. A cert opens a conversation; the portfolio closes the role. Most people get the order backwards and stall.

Truth 02

Technical English beats perfect English.

You don't need a flawless accent. You need to defend an architecture decision and tell the impact of your work in English. Plenty of strong people sabotage themselves waiting to feel "fluent enough".

Truth 03

Applying to open roles is the slowest path.

The best opportunities come through network, referrals and a LinkedIn that pulls the recruiter to you — not from mass-sending resumes into the void. It's positioning, not luck.

Truth 04

Your LinkedIn is your real resume.

Recruiters find you before they ever see a PDF. If your profile doesn't tell a clear story of value, you're invisible — no matter how good you are. This is where most people lose the role without knowing it.
