The three blockers this program tackles

It's not the technical knowledge that's missing. It's what happens in the moment — and that's exactly where this program works.

Nerves

Repetition with feedback until the interview feels like routine, not a surprise.

Forgetting

A written, rehearsed bank of stories. You don't improvise — you remember.

English

Interview-specific English: your answers, and the phrases that save the moment.

What you take away

  • A bank of 4 to 6 STAR stories in English, ready for behavioural questions
  • An interview phrase kit in English: buy time, ask for clarification, structure your answer
  • Live SQL practice and pipeline design, thinking out loud
  • Using AI tools in the Data Engineer's day-to-day — and how to talk about it in interviews

Frequently asked questions

Is this for someone just starting in Data Engineering?

It's for junior or mid-level folks who already have the technical foundation and need to turn that into clear interview answers — it's not a fundamentals course.

Do I need to be fluent in English?

You don't need to be fluent, but sessions are conducted in English, because that's the language of the real interview you'll face.

How does payment work?

A single payment of AUD 1,500. We'll confirm the details on the initial call.

How is this different from the Career Mentoring program?

Career Mentoring covers strategy, portfolio and LinkedIn over 6 weeks. Interview Ready is focused specifically on interview prep for Data Engineers, in English, over 8 sessions.
