Executive track

Better decisions for the business

For C-Level leaders, board directors, Senior Managers and Managers who need to turn technology into competitive advantage with clear priorities, risks and returns.

  • Strategic management: connect technology to corporate strategy, prioritise investment and define impact metrics.
  • Business focus: translate Cloud, Data and AI into operational efficiency, growth, customer experience and data-informed decisions.
  • Team building: design structure, roles, capabilities and rituals for high-performing teams.

Technical track

Technical capability that delivers

For technical leaders, architects, engineers and squads that need stronger decisions and sustainable, secure platforms ready to scale.

  • Develop your team: identify gaps, create growth plans and establish practices for autonomy, quality and collaboration.
  • Cloud infrastructure: architecture, security, reliability, observability, cost management and cloud modernisation.
